# 0043. The app icon is an SVG, rendered when the bundle is built

- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Status:** accepted

## Context

MusicPlayer.app had no icon, so the Dock, Finder and the app switcher showed the generic one. The
owner went through eleven rounds of sketches and picked "Halves": a beamed pair of notes, half
again its natural size so it runs off the tile, on a `#24d4f8` tile with a sheet of paper laid
over the left side. The note is black on the paper and white on the tile, and the white half sits
lower, so the note steps where it crosses the sheet's edge. The paper fades only faintly toward
the cyan at the bottom, a quarter of the fade in Searcher's and Simple Spotlight's icons. Those
two apps set the family: Apple's tile shape, paper and ink, and a glyph that slips where it is
cut.

The bundle is put together by `scripts/bundle`, not Xcode, so there is no asset catalog. The
2015 MacBook Pro (macOS 12) builds the app itself, through `scripts/pull`.

## Decision

`Support/AppIcon.svg` is the only copy of the icon, beside `Info.plist`. `scripts/bundle` has
Quick Look (`qlmanage -t`) render it once at 1024 px, scales that with `sips` to the ten sizes
an `.iconset` holds, packs them with `iconutil` into `Contents/Resources/AppIcon.icns`, and
`CFBundleIconFile` in `Info.plist` names it. All three tools ship with macOS. The script fails
if Quick Look writes nothing.

The SVG draws the tile at Apple's 824 px shape on a 1024 px canvas, so it lines up with other
apps' icons. The paper is opaque, its tint mixed into its color, and is drawn straight over the
white note. A see-through sheet needs the tile color under it to hide the white note, and two
shapes cut along the same edge left a thin cyan seam where each blended its own soft edge; the
owner saw it in the sketches.

## Alternatives considered

- **`sips` straight from the SVG at each size**, as Searcher and Simple Spotlight do. `sips` on
  macOS 12 reads SVG but writes nothing and exits 0, so the 2015 could no longer build the
  bundle. Rendering one master and scaling it gives the same icon on both Macs.
- **Check in the `.icns`.** A binary that has to be regenerated by hand whenever the SVG changes,
  and nothing would notice when they diverged.
- **`rsvg-convert` or another renderer.** A new dependency, for what macOS already does.

## Consequences

Editing the icon means editing the SVG; the next `./scripts/bundle` or `./scripts/dev` picks it
up. The small sizes are scaled from the 1024 px master rather than drawn at their size. The
sketches that led to the icon are not in the repo.
